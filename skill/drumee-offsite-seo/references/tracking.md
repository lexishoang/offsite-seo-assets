Use the link exactly as written (the user reads these parameters in analytics). One body link to Drumee plus the offer line; the blog.drumee.com link stays as before.

### Offer line
Place it as its own paragraph directly above the disclosure line. Keep the facts exactly: $1 first month, then $29/month; up to 10 members, 100 GB, guest access, secure/expiring links; first 100 startups; ends 30 November 2026; code TEAM1_BLOG. It is a SaaS-only offer, so the line never mentions self-hosting, and it never mentions audit logs or advanced admin (those are Business plan).

- Dev.to (neutral voice): `For readers who want to try it: Drumee's Team plan is **$1 for the first month**, then $29/month, for up to 10 members with 100 GB, guest access and secure, expiring share links. The offer is open to the first 100 startups until 30 November 2026 with code **TEAM1_BLOG**: [claim the $1 month](<devto link>).`
- Medium / Hashnode (first person): `If you want to try it yourself: Drumee's Team plan is **$1 for the first month**, then $29/month, for up to 10 members with 100 GB, guest access and secure, expiring share links. It's open to the first 100 startups until 30 November 2026 with code **TEAM1_BLOG**: [claim the $1 month](<platform link>).`

### After the campaign ends
Posts aired after 30 November 2026 get no offer line, and their Drumee link goes back to `https://drumee.com/?utm_source=<platform>&utm_medium=offsite&utm_campaign=seo_2026`. Ask the user whether a new campaign link or code replaces it, and update this file when they give one.
